Finding the best spot for the receiver

EE4002D FYP · Rotatable antenna · Updated 5 Oct 2026

We moved the receiving antenna to 9,261 positions and measured the SNR at each one. That took 16.8 hours. The best spot was 20 cm in front of the transmitter, at 50.45 dB.

1Setup

  • Tx antenna. Fixed. Sends the RF signal.
  • Rx antenna. Sits on the motorised stage. We step it through a 21 × 21 × 21 grid, 1.9 cm apart. Each dot is one stop.
  • Best spot. (0, −19.05, −15.24) cm. Straight in line with the Tx, same height.

2All 9,261 points

Colour is SNR. Drag to rotate.

3One height at a time

Same data, sliced flat. The bright patch shows up at Z = −15.24 cm. That's the Tx height.

4Still climbing at the edge

Closer to the Tx means a stronger peak. But it never levels off inside our grid, so the real peak may sit even closer.

Next: extend the sweep in −Y, towards the Tx.

5A faster search

Sweeping everything is slow. Coarse-to-fine checks a few spread-out points, zooms in on the best one, and repeats. It found the same spot within 0.1 dB.
